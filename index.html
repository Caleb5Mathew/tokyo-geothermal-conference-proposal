Designed around the Tokyo route.

Keep each day in one building

Both dates are reserved for the full conference day. All sessions, breaks and discussion blocks are planned at that day's venue; no midday cross-city transfers.

East first, west second

Asakusa matches November 21's east-Tokyo focus. Shibuya matches November 22's west-Tokyo focus. Nearby sightseeing would be separate from the conference program.

Before confirming

Check room availability, weekend access, setup time, pricing, catering, accessibility and AV with both venues. Dates, hours and rooms require venue agreement.
